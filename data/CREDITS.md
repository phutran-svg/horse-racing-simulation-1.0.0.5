# Data credits

* **Real horse pedigrees and records** (`realped.json`) are built from the open-horse-data project
  (https://github.com/jon-makinen/open-horse-data), sheets edition supplied by the player.
  Its licence requires this credit:
  **Sheep Meadow Road Games** and **Horse Racing Manager** — https://store.steampowered.com/app/2740170/Horse_Racing_Manager/
  The underlying sources (Wikidata CC0, Wikipedia CC BY-SA, racing authorities) keep their own terms.
  A small table of well-documented ancestors was added by hand where the data had gaps.
* **Jockeys and trainers** (`jockeys.csv`, `trainers.csv`): the player's own export from netkeiba.
* Stud fees 2026: Shadai Stallion Station, Coolmore, Darley, Juddmonte, Aga Khan Studs, Lane's End, Spendthrift,
  Rex Stud and Australian studs (2025 lists), via Racing Post, TDN, Breednet and TTR AusNZ reports.
* Sales figures: JBBA "Summary of Thoroughbred Sales in Japan for 2025" (japanracing.jp), JRHA and HBA reports.
